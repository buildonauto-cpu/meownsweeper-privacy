Privacy Policy

Meownsweeper: Cat Logic Puzzle

Updated: 20 October 2026 · Publisher: buildonauto

Meownsweeper works offline and requires no account. Game statistics and preferences are stored on your device. We do not collect or share your gameplay records.

Information stored on your device

The game uses Android DataStore to save:

  • Best completion times and the number of games played and completed.
  • Daily puzzle completion dates and streaks.
  • Sound, automatic marking and reminder preferences.
  • In-game rating and review-prompt status.

These records support gameplay, statistics, preferences and local reminders.

Collection and sharing

The offline app does not send these records to the publisher or third-party servers. It does not use advertising, analytics or tracking services. No registration or login is required.

Android backup and device transfer

Android may back up or transfer app data according to your device and Google account settings. The publisher does not operate that service or receive those backups. Manage backup and device-transfer options in your device or Google account settings.

Notifications

If you enable reminders and grant notification permission, daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can disable them in the game or in Android notification settings.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until cleared or removed by Android. To delete them, open Android Settings → Apps → Meownsweeper → Storage → Clear storage. The wording may vary by device. This resets game records and settings. Manage any Android backups separately. There is no publisher account to delete.

Support and external services

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond to your request. Do not send sensitive information unless necessary. Google Play and your email provider process information under their own policies.

Children

The game does not request personal information from players. Local gameplay storage works in the same way for all players.

This policy webpage

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information about visits under its privacy statement. We have added no analytics, tracking scripts or cookies to this page.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its revision date if the app's data practices change.
